About Me

I’m Goran, a photographer fascinated by the wild beauty of Africa, especially the Masai Mara. “Whispers of the Mara” is my visual diary capturing the elegance of wildlife, the silence of vast landscapes, and fleeting moments of life in the savannah.

My work transforms safari experiences into timeless images, telling stories of nature, emotion, and the poetry hidden in every frame.
