IMAGO BY GORUS

About Me

I’m Goran, a photographer fascinated by stillness, emotion, and light.

My work is an ongoing exploration of quiet spaces — from ancient streets and forgotten corners to vast landscapes where time seems to stop.

“Imago by Gorus” is my visual journal — a gallery of journeys, emotions, and the beauty hidden between moments.
