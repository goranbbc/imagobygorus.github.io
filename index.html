The Raw Wilderness

Masai Mara, Kenya | June 04 – June 11

8 days in the heart of Masai Mara. We tracked the first light across the golden savannah, positioning our vehicles for precise angle framing, natural backlit silhouettes, and clean fine art compositions.

From 5:00 AM game drives to mid-day portfolio reviews and image breakdown sessions at camp — this expedition was built around pure field immersion and visual storytelling.
